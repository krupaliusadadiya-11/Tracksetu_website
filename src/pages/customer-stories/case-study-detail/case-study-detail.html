Building a Connected Operational System for a Tile Business

SAGA CERA is a tile and sanitary business managing multiple connected activities across quotations, orders, inventory, purchasing, and payments.

TrackSetu's first customer implementation was shaped around these operational requirements, bringing these workflows together into a more structured and connected system.

SAGA CERA's daily operations involve several processes that depend on one another. Managing these processes as a connected workflow was an important requirement for the implementation.

The Business Need

A typical business process can move through several stages:

Quotation → Order → Inventory → Purchasing → Payment

Each stage is connected to the next. The requirement was to create a system where these activities could be managed as part of one operational flow rather than as isolated processes.

The key areas included:

  • Quotations — managing customer requirements and quotations
  • Orders — connecting confirmed requirements to operations
  • Inventory — managing product and stock-related information
  • Purchasing — connecting purchasing requirements with operations
  • Payments — keeping the final stage connected to the overall workflow

The TrackSetu Approach

TrackSetu was shaped around SAGA CERA's actual operational workflow.

Instead of treating each business activity as a separate function, the implementation connects the different stages of the process:

Quotation → Order → Inventory → Purchasing → Payment

This creates a more structured flow of information across the business and provides a centralized foundation for managing related activities.

What Became Possible

The implementation provides a foundation for:

  • Connected workflows across key business activities
  • Centralized information within one system
  • Better operational visibility across processes
  • Less repeated data entry between connected activities
  • More structured day-to-day operations
